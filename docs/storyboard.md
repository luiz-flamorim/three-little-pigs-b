# Storyboard

Twelve scenes. Each beat has one caption. Timings are at 1× speed (the speed control offers 0.75×–2×). The pigs are customers in every scene.

| # | Scene | What the viewer sees | Key message |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Customers** | The three pigs as one customer group; their request appears in a speech card; budget, timeframe and the (fictional) safety standard; eight requirements arrive as sticky notes in two waves. | “The customers define WHAT they need. The AI construction company determines HOW the work is completed.” |
| 2 | **The AI Construction Company** | A request packet travels from the customers to the company; a WORK ACCEPTED stamp lands; the team appears (Orchestrator, six specialists, inspector, human); a gold guardrail boundary draws around them. | A coordinated system of specialists, not one giant chatbot. |
| 3 | **The Orchestrator** | The goal card and the Orchestrator; the workflow strip lights up UNDERSTAND → PLAN → DELEGATE → PASS CONTEXT → MONITOR → VALIDATE → REWORK → COMBINE; eight task cards appear, then their assigned agents, then their dependencies (“waits for approved #2”). | “The Orchestrator does not build the house. It coordinates the system that builds the house.” |
| 4 | **The Specialist Agents** | A plain definition of an AI agent; six agent cards highlight one by one (input → output, next destination, guardrail); then the Validation Agent and Human Reviewer, marked as separate from the specialists. | Each agent has one job, defined inputs and outputs. |
| 5 | **Without Orchestration** | Same agents, no orchestrator, no validation, no human, no guardrails (ghost outlines). Ten steps: independent start, duplicate research, builder starts early with straw, conflicting assumptions, finance costs an outdated design, risk warning ignored, no checks, writer marks incomplete work final, the Wolf exploits the weak door, the house collapses. Nodes drift further out of place each step. | “Many agents are active. No coordinated outcome exists.” Outcome: UNSAFE HOUSE · CUSTOMER REQUIREMENTS NOT MET · WORK MUST BE REDONE. The system design is the problem, not the agents. |
| 6 | **With Orchestration** | Fourteen traceable hand-offs inside the guardrail boundary. Each shows a moving packet and a hand-off card (what, from, to, validated?, if it fails, guardrail). Includes Design v1 FAIL → rework → v2 PASS, Finance and Risk in parallel on the same approved version, a HIGH chimney risk escalated to the human, build authorisation, final validation, Writer documents, human approves, final check, delivery. With the Wolf on, attempts are blocked at the boundary or detected. | Ordered, traceable, validated. Outcome: SAFE HOUSE · WITHIN BUDGET · KNOWN RISKS ADDRESSED · VALIDATION PASSED · HUMAN APPROVED · SAFE TO OCCUPY. |
| 7 | **Guardrails and Validation** | The 14 guardrail rules around the agents; a breach attempt (unapproved website) makes the boundary shake red and is blocked. The Building Inspector runs 11 checks on Design v1; one fails; correction instructions go to the Architecture Agent; the rework loop animates; v2 passes. | “Guardrails do not perform the work…” and “Producing an answer is not the same as producing an approved answer.” |
| 8 | **The Wolf** | The Wolf outside the boundary; seven dotted red attempts in turn: fake evidence, prompt injection, impersonation, a too-cheap straw offer, sabotaged delivery, an attempt on the Orchestrator, huffing at the finished house. Each shows the control and the evidence left behind; alerts accumulate. | “The Wolf attacks the system. The Wolf is not part of the legitimate construction team.” |
| 9 | **Insider Threat (optional)** | Customer C (still a customer) is approached through a separate rose dotted channel outside the company. Twelve steps: approach, others unaware, information shared, leverage, refusal, threat, monitoring alert, work paused, access restricted, escalation, rework assessed (re-key locks), customers informed. A status panel and incident timeline follow along. | A security event affecting the system, not a judgement of character. |
| 10 | **Human Oversight** | An escalation queue: chimney risk (REQUEST CHANGES), straw offer (REJECT), missing brick certificate (RETURN TO ORCHESTRATOR), possible exposure (STOP THE WORKFLOW, insider scenario only), final proposal (APPROVE). The matching “mandatory when” condition highlights. Viewers can try any decision button to see its effect. | Validation checks the work; a human stays accountable. |
| 11 | **Final Check** | The Orchestrator ticks ten completeness items with evidence; the delivery lock opens only when all ten are confirmed. | Only then may the outcome be delivered to the pigs. |
| 12 | **The Outcome** | The house assembles (foundation, brick walls, reinforced door, roof and chimney, chimney guard); a packet hands it to the pigs; the outcome badges; the seven-line key message; the closing tagline and the fictional-model note. | “SAME STORY. A NEW WAY TO UNDERSTAND AI.” |
