# 90-second narration script

For a live demo, play Scenes 1, 5, 6, 8 and 12 at 2× speed, or click through scene by scene while reading. About 230 words, read at a steady pace.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Scene 1 · The Customers | “Meet the Three Little Pigs. They are customers, not AI agents. They need a safe house that keeps out the Wolf, stays within budget and arrives on time.” |
| 0:10–0:18 | Scene 2 · The Company | “They hire an AI construction company. The pigs say WHAT they need; the company decides HOW.” |
| 0:18–0:28 | Scene 3 · The Orchestrator | “Inside, an Orchestrator acts as an invisible project manager. It doesn't build anything. It plans the work, hands each task to the right specialist and passes on the right information.” |
| 0:28–0:36 | Scene 4 · The Agents | “Each agent has one job: research, design, cost, risk, build and write.” |
| 0:36–0:48 | Scene 5 · Without Orchestration | “Without coordination, watch what happens. Everyone starts at once, work is duplicated, the builder starts before there's a design, and a risk warning is ignored. Lots of activity, but the house fails.” |
| 0:48–1:02 | Scenes 6–7 · With Orchestration | “Now with orchestration. Every hand-off is tracked and checked. When design version one fails inspection, it goes back to the architect with clear instructions. Guardrails set the boundaries. Nothing moves on until it passes.” |
| 1:02–1:12 | Scenes 8–9 · The Wolf | “The Wolf attacks from outside with fake evidence, hidden instructions and swapped materials. Each attempt is blocked or detected. Even when a customer account is compromised, monitoring spots it and access is restricted.” |
| 1:12–1:22 | Scenes 10–11 · Oversight | “High-risk decisions go to a human, who stays accountable. A final check confirms everything is complete.” |
| 1:22–1:30 | Scene 12 · Outcome | “Only then is the safe house delivered. Same story. A new way to understand AI.” |
